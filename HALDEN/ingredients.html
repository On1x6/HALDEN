Ingredients, explained

The actives we rely on, what they do and where you’ll find them.

Niacinamide

Calms and evens tone

A form of vitamin B3 that reduces the look of redness and pores. We use 2%, an amount that works without irritating.

Barrier Calm Serum

Ceramides

Strengthen the barrier

Fats that already exist in healthy skin. Replacing them helps skin hold water and resist irritation.

Barrier Calm Serum

Gluconolactone (PHA)

Gentle exfoliation

Its large molecules sit on the surface, so it smooths texture slowly with very little stinging.

Clear Tone Exfoliant

Hyaluronic acid

Holds moisture

Can bind many times its weight in water, giving skin a plump, cushioned feel.

Cloud Barrier Cream

Bakuchiol

Plant alternative to retinol

Supports smoother-looking skin overnight without the dryness, and is suitable during pregnancy.

Night Repair Balm

Zinc oxide

Mineral sun protection

Sits on the skin and reflects UVA and UVB. Our non-nano grade is reef-safe.
