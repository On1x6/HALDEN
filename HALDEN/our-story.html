Less, but better.

Halden began with one question: why does a good routine need fifteen steps?

We started in 2019 after a dermatologist told our founder, Mara, that her irritated skin needed fewer products, not more. We looked for formulas with short ingredient lists, proven actives and no fragrance. We couldn’t find them, so we made them.

Every Halden product earns its place. If an ingredient doesn’t do a clear job for your skin, it doesn’t go in the bottle.

How we got here

  1. 2019: A kitchen-table formula

    Barrier Calm Serum is tested on friends and family, then refined over 14 batches.

  2. 2021: Dermatologist partnership

    Our formulas are clinically patch-tested on sensitive skin for the first time.

  3. 2023: Refillable glass

    We launch refill pouches that use 70% less plastic than a new bottle.

  4. 2026: Six essentials

    A complete routine, from cleanser to SPF, and nothing extra.
